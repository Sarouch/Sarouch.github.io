# Research Portfolio — Scientific Machine Learning, Computer Vision & Physics

**Sarah Chouchene, Ph.D.**  
*AI/ML Research Engineer | Computer Vision | Scientific Imaging | Physics-Informed AI*

Welcome to my research portfolio! I am an AI/ML research engineer with a Ph.D. in Plasma Physics and Machine Vision from the University of Lorraine, France. My research focuses on machine learning and computer vision methods for understanding complex physical systems.

My work spans high-speed scientific imaging, plasma turbulence analysis, magnetic sensor localization, physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), 3D reconstruction, and efficient deep learning. I am interested in translating scientific methods into practical AI applications, including multimodal learning, sensor systems, and edge AI.

## Selected Research Projects

### 1. High-Speed Imaging and Plasma Turbulence Analysis

**Ph.D. research | Institut Jean Lamour — CNRS, University of Lorraine**

**Objective:** Detect, track, and characterize turbulent structures in fusion plasmas.

- Developed supervised deep-learning methods for detecting turbulent plasma structures.
- Applied object detection and tracking to experimental imaging data, including sequences acquired at up to **1 million frames per second**.
- Investigated plasma filament dynamics and interactions using quantitative image analysis.
- Combined experimental diagnostics, image processing, and machine learning.

**Technologies:** Python, computer vision, deep learning, YOLO, image processing.

**Related publications:** *Physical Review E* (2024); *Scientific Reports* (2024).

### 2. Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Magnetic Sensor Localization

**Research Engineer | ICube / GAIA — CNRS, University of Strasbourg**

**Objective:** Estimate magnetic sensor position and orientation from magnetic-field measurements.

- Developed physics-informed learning approaches for magnetic localization.
- Implemented Python/PyTorch training and inference workflows with CUDA acceleration.
- Used MLflow for experiment tracking, metrics, and model artifacts.
- Compared model predictions with Radia magnetic-field simulations.

**Technologies:** Python, PyTorch, CUDA, PINNs, MLflow, Radia.

**Research output:** Manuscript on PINNs and Random Forest for magnetic localization, listed as submitted in my academic CV.

### 3. 3D Reconstruction for Custom Orthosis Design

**Research Engineer | ICube / GAIA**

**Objective:** Develop a 3D reconstruction pipeline for customized orthopedic devices.

- Developed preprocessing, segmentation, surface and mesh reconstruction, and quality-control workflows.
- Integrated reconstructed outputs into clinical prototyping and visualization tools.

**Technologies:** Python, OpenCV, scikit-image, Open3D, CloudCompare, ParaView.

*Some implementation details and data are subject to project confidentiality.*

### 4. Efficient Object Detection Through Knowledge Distillation

**Research Engineer | ICube / GAIA**

**Objective:** Investigate compact object detectors for resource-constrained environments.

- Applied teacher–student knowledge distillation to YOLO models.
- Explored accuracy–efficiency trade-offs for embedded and edge applications.

**Technologies:** Python, PyTorch, YOLO, knowledge distillation.

### 5. COMPASS Tokamak Experimental Diagnostics

**Research Internship | Institute of Plasma Physics, Prague**

- Processed experimental diagnostic datasets.
- Contributed to analysis, interpretation, and validation of plasma measurements.

**Research areas:** Plasma physics, experimental diagnostics, scientific data analysis.
